Folio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23

This policy applies to the Folio iOS app, the Folio web version, and public web pages such as this privacy policy and the support page. It explains what information they handle, how they handle it, how long it is kept, and how you can manage it.

1. Summary

  • Folio requires no sign-up or login. There are no accounts.
  • The app has no server of its own and does not upload your saves, settings, feedback records or usage to us.
  • Your saves, settings, reading history and messages are stored on your own device (and may be included in your device backups; see section 11). Deleting the app (or clearing site data for the web version) removes them from your device.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your personal information and do not profile you.
  • The iOS app's interface, fonts and content are all bundled in the app, and it connects to the network only in the cases listed below. The web version loads its files only from the Folio website itself (hosted on GitHub Pages; see section 6), not fonts or scripts from Google or any other site.
  • Content updates: when the app starts, returns to the foreground or checks for updates, it downloads the latest resource data (a public file; see section 8) from Alibaba Cloud Object Storage Service (OSS), so newly added resources appear without updating the app. This request sends none of your personal information.
  • Apart from the data Apple provides to developers (see section 6), we receive your information only when you email us yourself.

2. Who we are

Folio is developed and run by an individual developer: the developer named on Folio's App Store page. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Folio.

Contact email: 1421718903@qq.com. In the app, you can also find it under Me → Help & support → Contact us.

3. Data on your device

To remember your choices, the app stores the following on your device. The iOS app keeps it in the app's own storage; the web version keeps it in the local storage (localStorage) your browser provides for the website's domain. The app does not automatically send any of it to us or to any third party (for device backups, see section 11).

  • Settings and saves: the sections you chose (My sections), the selected industry, sort order, density, language, article text size, haptics setting and daily reminder setting and time, plus the list of resources you have saved. Stored when you save or unsave a resource or change these settings.
  • Reading history and follows: the articles you have opened, how far you read and whether you finished them (for Continue reading and unread marks; at most the 200 most recent), your Read later list, and the products you follow in Following together with the articles you have already seen for each.
  • Home Screen widget (iOS app): the app passes the titles, IDs and sections of a few of today's top stories to the Folio widget, stored in storage shared by the app and the widget on your device (an App Group). It never leaves your device.
  • Appearance: whether you chose light or dark mode. Nothing is stored when you choose System.
  • App tour: whether you have seen the first-launch tour (noted once you finish or skip it, so it does not open again by itself).
  • Messages: content update notices, daily picks (the IDs of that day's recommended resources and the industry selected at the time), the read status of messages and notices, and the data version and resource IDs used to tell whether new resources have been added. Each message records when it was created, so daily picks also record when you first opened or returned to the app on each day you used it (only the 7 most recent are kept, i.e. the last 7 days on which you used the app).
  • Feedback records: each time you submit a report (Report a problem), the app keeps a full copy of the report in Messages (see section 4), including the details and any contact information you entered, so you can copy it again later. These records are kept for a long time; see section 11.
  • Content data: the resource data most recently downloaded (see section 8), so the app still works offline. It is a copy of a public file and contains nothing about you.

Search terms and unsubmitted report forms are kept only temporarily while you use them and are not saved. The app does not use cookies and does not create or store any identifier for you or your device.

The app also reads your system language, light/dark and Reduce Motion settings to adjust the interface. The app does not store or send these settings.

When you use the web version, your browser may also cache its web files. This cache is managed by the browser and contains no personal information.

4. Reports and contacting us

You can tell us that a resource is gone, has wrong information or infringes rights, or send us suggestions, via Me → Help & support → Report a problem, or via “Something wrong? Report it” on a resource's detail page.

When you submit, the app creates a report text containing: the report type; the name and ID of the resource you selected, or the resource name you typed; your details; the contact information you chose to give; the data version; and the date and time on your device when you submitted.

The app does not send this text anywhere itself. The note below the form says which of two ways is used: either the text is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it into an email to 1421718903@qq.com yourself, or a pre-filled email opens in the mail app on your device and you decide whether to send it.

On the app's Contact us page, you can tap Copy email to copy our email address, or Write an email to open your mail app. An email opened with Write an email is pre-filled with the app version, data version, system type (such as iOS) and interface language to help us troubleshoot. This contains no personal information, and you can edit or delete it before sending. You can also tap Troubleshooting info to copy just these items.

If you email us, we receive the email's contents and any attachments such as screenshots or screen recordings, along with your email address, sender name and information your mail app or email service adds automatically (such as the time sent and your email signature; some email services also include the sender's IP address in the email headers).

We use this information only to handle the issue you reported (including correcting or removing listings and handling rights complaints), to consider your suggestions for improving Folio, and to reply to you, and for no other purpose.

When handling a rights complaint, we may ask you for proof of rights or authorization, which may include your name, organization or identity details. We use it only to verify the complaint and keep it for the period described in section 11. Unless truly necessary, please do not send sensitive personal information such as ID numbers. The Folio app itself never asks you for sensitive personal information.

Contact information is optional. Please include only what is needed to handle the issue. When you fill it in, iOS or your browser may suggest your own email address; it is entered only if you tap the suggestion.

5. Sharing and the clipboard

When you tap Share, you can send an image or a link. What is shared is only the article or resource name, Folio's summary, the source name and a link that opens the Folio share page (see section 8); the share image is drawn on your device and shows the same content plus a QR code (which opens the same share page). None of this contains personal information, and you decide in the system share sheet who receives it and whether to save the image to your photos. If your device does not support the system share sheet, the text is copied to the clipboard instead.

The app writes to the clipboard only when you tap a matching button (Share, Copy link, Send feedback, Copy again, Copy email, Troubleshooting info) and never reads the clipboard.

Please note: what is on the clipboard can be read by other apps (when an app reads it on its own, iOS usually asks you first), and if Handoff is on, Universal Clipboard may also make it appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If a report includes your contact information, that is copied too.

6. Third-party services

The iOS app uses the open-source framework Capacitor to package the web content as an app. It runs only on your device and does not collect or send any data. Apart from this, the app includes no other third-party SDKs, and no analytics, advertising, push, login or crash-reporting SDKs. The open-source interface fonts, Geist and JetBrains Mono, are also bundled with the app and the web version rather than loaded from a font service such as Google Fonts.

The daily reminder, the Home Screen widget and Read aloud use features built into iOS (local notifications, widgets and speech), which run on your device and do not go through us or any server. In the web version, Read aloud uses your browser's built-in speech: Safari speaks on your device; some desktop browsers (such as Chrome) offer online voices produced by the browser maker, and what is read aloud is only the public article text, with no personal information.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. The services below receive some information in specific situations and handle it under their own privacy policies:

GitHub Pages (GitHub): The Folio web version, this privacy policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit these pages, GitHub receives and may log standard access information such as your IP address, browser type, referring page and the page requested.

GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Alibaba Cloud Object Storage Service (OSS): the resource data file used by the app and the web version is stored on Alibaba Cloud OSS, on servers in Hong Kong, China. When the app or the web version downloads it, Alibaba Cloud receives and may log standard access information such as your IP address, device and browser type, the time of access and the file requested.

Alibaba Cloud privacy policy: https://terms.aliyun.com/legal-agreement/terms/suit_bu1_ali_cloud/suit_bu1_ali_cloud201902141711_54837.html

Apple: The Folio iOS app is distributed through the App Store, and Apple handles downloads and updates under its own privacy policy. If you have turned on Share With App Developers under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone or iPad, Apple may provide us with Folio crash reports, diagnostics and aggregated usage statistics (such as active devices, sessions and retention). You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

In addition, whether or not this option is on, Apple provides developers with aggregated App Store data that does not identify individuals (such as downloads, country or region, and device type). We can also see ratings and reviews you post publicly on the App Store, and may reply publicly. We use the information Apple provides only to understand how the app runs and is used, to find and fix problems, and to improve Folio.

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Email provider (Tencent QQ Mail): We receive email with QQ Mail, so emails you send us are stored on Tencent's servers and handled by Tencent under its privacy policy.

Tencent privacy policy: https://privacy.qq.com/

The third-party services we chose (GitHub Pages, Alibaba Cloud OSS and Tencent QQ Mail) provide the same or equal protection of user information as described in this policy. Other than the cases above and where required by law, we do not share your personal information with any third party or disclose it publicly.

If Folio is ever handed over to another developer, we will tell you the recipient's name and contact details in the app or on this page, and require them to keep handling your information under this policy; if they want to change why or how it is handled, they must ask for your consent again.

7. Purposes and legal basis

We process information only for the purposes below:

  • Data on your device: to remember your settings, saves and messages and provide the features you use. It is processed only on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Emails you send us: to handle issues, consider suggestions and reply to you. Basis: this is necessary to respond to the request you chose to send us.
  • Proof in rights complaints: to verify the complaint. Basis: this is necessary to handle the complaint and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Web access records: received by GitHub when serving the pages. Basis: this is necessary to provide the web pages to you.
  • Access information when downloading the resource data: received by Alibaba Cloud when serving this public file. The data is downloaded so you see newly added and corrected resources without updating the app. Basis: this is necessary to provide the app's content to you.
  • Diagnostics and usage statistics from Apple: to understand how the app runs and is used, find and fix problems and improve Folio. Basis: the choice (consent) you made in iOS Settings, which you can turn off at any time.

If we want to use information for a purpose not listed above, we will update this policy first, and where your consent is required, we will ask for it first.

8. Web version and public pages

The web version handles data the same way as the app: settings, saves and messages are stored in the local storage your browser provides for the website's domain and are not sent to us. Clearing the website's data in your browser settings deletes them.

Content updates: Folio's resource data (public content such as resource names, categories, summaries, highlights and learning guides) is kept in a public file on Alibaba Cloud Object Storage Service (OSS). The app and the web version download this file when they start, when they return from the background (if more than 30 minutes have passed since the last check), and when you tap Check or pull down to refresh; new content is shown right away, without updating the app. This request only downloads that public file: it carries no cookies and sends none of your saves, settings or any other personal information. As with any ordinary web page, Alibaba Cloud receives access information such as your IP address (see section 6). The most recently downloaded data is kept on your device so it still works offline.

Share page: when someone opens a link you shared, or scans the QR code on a share image, they see the share page on the Folio website (hosted on GitHub Pages). It shows only public content such as the article or resource title, summary and highlights, plus an App Store button, read from the same public data file on Alibaba Cloud OSS. The link contains only the article ID (and interface language) and nothing about you; as with any web page, GitHub and Alibaba Cloud receive standard access information such as the visitor's IP address (see section 6). The share page uses no cookies and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

This privacy policy and the support page are static pages. They use no cookies, contain no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and do not load fonts, scripts or other resources from any third-party site.

9. What the app does not do

  • It requires no account and collects no registration details such as your name or phone number.
  • It contains no ads and no analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking tools.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • It does not sell or rent your personal information, does not profile you, and makes no automated decisions based on your information.
  • It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files, and does not read your photos. Only if you choose Save Image in the share sheet does iOS, with your permission, add that share image to your photo library.
  • It uses no push service, and there is no server sending you messages. The daily reminder is a local notification you turn on yourself: at the time you choose, iOS shows a fixed short reminder on your device, with no network connection. The first time you turn it on, iOS asks whether to allow notifications; you can turn it off at any time in Me → Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Folio. Everything in Messages is also generated on your device.
  • It uses no cookies and does not read the clipboard.

10. Where data is located

We (the developer) are based in mainland China, and emails you send us are mainly read and handled by us in mainland China. Emails are stored on Tencent's QQ Mail servers; the servers of GitHub and Apple may be located outside your country or region.

The resource data file is stored on Alibaba Cloud servers in Hong Kong, China. When the app or the web version downloads it, access information such as your IP address is sent there.

If you email us from the EU or a similar region, your email will be transferred to mainland China to be handled. Mainland China is not among the countries or regions the EU recognizes as having an adequate level of data protection.

Data saved on your device stays on your device, unless you copy, share or send it yourself, or it is included in a backup of your device (see section 11).

11. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it. Messages keeps at most the 60 most recent messages and removes older ones automatically; of these, only the 7 most recent daily picks are kept. Reading history keeps at most the 200 most recent articles.

In practice, feedback records (including any contact information you entered) stay on your device for a long time: daily picks do not push them out, and a record is removed automatically only after more than 50 newer content updates or reports have built up.

There is currently no one-tap button in the app to clear your data. You can unsave resources or change settings at any time. To delete everything (including feedback records), delete the Folio app on iOS, or clear the website's data in your browser settings for the web version.

On iOS, this data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups (depending on your backup settings) and may come back when you restore your device from a backup. The system may also clear it when your device is low on storage.

In the web version, some browsers may clear this data automatically if you have not visited the website for a while, for example Safari when you have not visited the website during 7 days of using Safari.

We keep emails you send us until the issue is resolved and we no longer need to be in touch with you, and then delete them. Emails and proof relating to rights complaints are kept until the matter is closed. Unless the law requires otherwise, you can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Information received by GitHub, Alibaba Cloud and Apple is kept according to their own policies.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, copy, correct, supplement and delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Folio has no accounts and most information is only on your device, so you can manage it directly:

  • Access: view your saves and settings under Me; view your feedback records in Messages (the bell at the top right).
  • Copy: in Messages, tap Copy again on a feedback record. Saves cannot currently be exported in one go; you can view or share them one at a time.
  • Correct: change language, appearance, density and other settings at any time under Me → Settings.
  • Delete: unsave resources, or delete the app or clear website data as described in section 11.
  • Withdraw consent: sharing, copying and sending reports each require an action from you every time, so you can stop using them at any time. The option to share diagnostics and usage statistics with developers can be turned off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Account deletion: Folio has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Deleting the app removes all data the app has stored.

We cannot see the data on your device and cannot tell who you are, so we cannot access or delete that data for you.

For information you have emailed us, you can write to 1421718903@qq.com to access, copy, correct, supplement or delete it, or to ask us to stop contacting you. You can also ask us to restrict or stop processing it, to explain this policy, or, where the conditions set by law are met, to transfer a copy of it to another processor you designate.

To confirm it is you, we may ask you to write from the address you originally used. We will handle and answer your request within a reasonable time, and if we cannot meet your request, we will explain why.

If you think we have handled your personal information improperly, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the personal information or data protection authority where you live.

13. Security

The app does not upload your data to our servers. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security measures, such as your passcode. We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access to emails you send us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

If a security incident involving your personal information occurs, we will inform you by email or other means as required by applicable law.

14. Minors

Folio is a tech news and learning-resource app for everyone. It requires no account and does not actively collect anyone's personal information, including that of minors.

If you are under 18, please read this policy under the guidance of a parent or other guardian.

If you are under 14 (or another age set by the law where you live), please read this policy with a parent or other guardian and get their permission before emailing us.

If we learn that we have received a child's personal information without a guardian's consent, we will delete it. Parents and guardians with questions can contact 1421718903@qq.com.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features change. The updated version will be published on this page and in the app, and the date at the top will be changed.

If a change involves collecting new personal information, changing how it is used or adding a new third-party service, we will point it out clearly in the app (for example in Messages) or on this page, and where your consent is required, we will ask for it first.
