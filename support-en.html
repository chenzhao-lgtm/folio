What to include

To help us find the problem faster, please include as much of the following as you can:

  • Device model, for example iPhone 15
  • iOS version: Settings → General → About → iOS Version
  • App version: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Folio
  • Data version: in the app, Me → Settings → Content updates, shown as “Version …”
  • If you use the web version, your browser's name and version
  • What you did before the problem appeared, and whether it happens every time
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if you can

If you tap Write an email in the app, the app version, data version, system and interface language are filled in for you, so you only need to add your device model, the steps and screenshots. You can also tap Troubleshooting info on the Contact us page to copy these items.

Please do not include personal information that is not related to the problem.

Where are my saves? Can I lose them?

Saves are stored only on the device you are using. You can find them under Me, and “View all” lets you search and filter them. Folio has no accounts, so saves do not sync between devices, and saves in the app and the web version are separate.

Saves are lost if you delete the app or clear website data. The iOS app keeps a second copy of your saves and settings in its own settings storage, so if the system clears the app's web data when the device is low on storage, they come back the next time you open the app. In the web version, Safari and some other browsers may clear your saves when the device is low on storage or if you do not open the Folio website for a long time. If you restore your device from an iCloud or computer backup that includes app data, your saves may be restored too. If a resource is taken down, it stays in your saves as a greyed-out “Removed” row that can't be opened; tap Remove to clear it from your saves.

How do I change the language?

Go to Me → Settings → Language and choose System, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese), English, 日本語 (Japanese), 한국어 (Korean), Deutsch (German) or Français (French). Each language is listed in its own script, and the small text on the Language row always says “Language” (“语言” in the English interface), so you can find it even if you picked the wrong language. With System, the app uses your device language if it is one of these, and English otherwise.

How do I switch light or dark mode?

Go to Me → Settings → Appearance and choose Light, Dark or System, or tap the light/dark button at the top right to switch directly.

How do I turn off vibration on taps?

The iPhone app vibrates lightly when you tap, switch or press and hold. You can turn this off under Me → Settings → Haptics. If System Haptics is off in the iPhone's Settings → Sounds & Haptics, the app does not vibrate either. The web version only vibrates briefly for long-press previews in browsers on Android phones, and you can turn that off in the same place.

Why are there no links to original sites?

Folio only recommends and organizes resources: it shows each resource's name, source and category, plus a summary, highlights and a learning guide written by Folio with AI assistance and reviewed by a person, and does not republish original content. The app does not show or open links to original sites. To see the original, search for the resource name and source name shown on the card.

Do you collect my data?

Folio needs no account. The app does not upload your saves, settings or usage to us and has no ads or analytics. Fonts are bundled in the app, and the app loads no fonts or scripts from Google or any other third-party site. We only receive information when you email us, or when you choose in iOS to share analytics with app developers. For full details, see the Privacy Policy (in the app: Me → Help & support → Privacy Policy).

When it starts and when you check for updates, the app downloads the latest resource data from Alibaba Cloud Object Storage Service (OSS), so newly added resources appear without updating the app. This only downloads a public file and sends none of your saves, settings or other personal information.
